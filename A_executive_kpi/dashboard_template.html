Metric definitions

New referred patientsDistinct patients whose record was created in the period. Business proxy for new referral intake.
Not order series (renewals, therapy changes) and not referral events (duplicates, re-referrals).
New treatment startsDistinct patients whose first qualifying visit (Complete, medication administered, at least one given line) falls in the period.
Derived from events; the system's first-appointment flag is shown only for comparison.
Completed treatment visitsDistinct appointments that were Complete with medication administered.
Split lots, add-on lines and duplicate lines never multiply a visit.
Booked visitsDistinct appointments after the snapshot with status Scheduled or Confirmed.
Pipeline only. Most visits beyond about three weeks are not booked yet.
Active / dormant providersActive: 1+ referral in (as-of - 90 days, as-of]. Dormant: earlier referral, none in that window.
Never-referred providers are counted separately. Calendar quarters are not a substitute for a rolling window.
Provider countyCounty of the referring provider's practice ZIP via the primary crosswalk row.
Not the patient's home county and not the infusion site's county.
